# O2: the survey by condition

> **The sprint's O2** ([a working note, not public]). Done when two matched pairs are chosen and every state is
> recorded twice, as deposited and as measured (SO-DOMAIN-05). Part 1, the structures and their deposited states, is
> done. Part 2, the gate, follows the criteria below, **committed before its first run**.

## The Problem This Solves

A state label read as fact was the first ChannelScope's deepest error. This survey finds every RyR1 structure of the
four species, records what its depositors call its state, and measures its pore at the gate, so that the pairs the test
uses (O3) are closed and open by measurement as well as by word.

## Part 2: the gate, criteria fixed before the first run

**The gate residue.** Rabbit I4937, "the hydrophobic gate residue" (des Georges et al. 2016; FR-16). The same residue in
the other numberings, found by its flanking sequence in each UniProt entry: human I4938 (as M01's mapping also gives),
mouse I4935 (E9PZQ0) and pig I4935 (P16960). A structure is measured only if it holds four RyR1 chains, each with an
isoleucine at that author number; otherwise it is refused, with the reason recorded.

**Two implementations, independent in parser, axis and measure:**

| | A | B |
|---|---|---|
| Parser | Biopython 1.88, `MMCIFParser` | gemmi, a separate library |
| The pore axis | Through the centroid of the four gate Cα atoms, normal to their best-fit plane | Through the centroid of the transmembrane region (the gate's residue −117 to +100, all four chains, heavy atoms), along its distinct principal axis of inertia |
| The measure | The least distance from the axis to any heavy atom of the four gate residues, less that atom's radius (C 1.70, N 1.55, O 1.52, S 1.80 Å) | A probe along the axis, 12 Å either side of the gate's height in 0.25 Å steps: at each point, the distance to the nearest heavy atom of the four chains, less its radius; the least of these within 3 Å of the gate's height |

**Classes,** this survey's convention, not a value from the literature: **closed** below 2.0 Å, **open** above 3.0 Å,
**indeterminate** between. Each structure's class is also given at thresholds 0.5 Å lower and higher, so a reader sees
which classes depend on the convention.

**Checks, each with what fails it:**

| Check | What it tests | It fails if |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | The two implementations agree | For any structure, their classes differ, or their radii differ by more than 1.0 Å. Each disagreement is listed |
| G2 | Positive controls from an independent source: 5TB0 is "a closed RyR1 pore" and 5T9V "an open RyR1" in Woll and Van Petegem 2022 (Section 1's quotations `woll2022_5tb0_closed`, `woll2022_5tb0_5t9v`) | Either is classed otherwise by either implementation. Then no measured state from this run is used |
| G3 | The record, not a pass or fail | — Each structure's deposited words beside its measured class; a disagreement is flagged and kept (SO-DOMAIN-05) |

**Planted faults, each of which must be caught:**
- 5TB0 with its gate atoms moved 4 Å further from the axis: both implementations must class it open.
- 5T9V with one RyR1 chain removed: both must refuse it.
- A structure whose gate residues are renamed from isoleucine: both must refuse it.

**Then the pairs.** O3's rule chooses the two pairs: matched (the table above), measured states agreeing with
deposited ones, no drug in one member only, different species and laboratories, highest resolution.
